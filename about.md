## Background

Cybersecurity and Network Engineer with 15+ years of progressive experience spanning offensive security, enterprise network architecture, and defense-sector systems integration. B.S. Computer Networks & Cybersecurity (UMGC, 2022) with a red team / offensive security focus. OSCP in progress. Proven track record leading incident response, hardening carrier-grade infrastructure, and operating in SCIF-adjacent defense environments. Bridges operations, engineering, and security to deliver resilient, mission-critical infrastructure.

U.S. Navy Veteran. Bilingual EN/ES.

## Career Arc

### Carrier-Grade Network Engineering *(2022 – Present)*

Architect and deploy enterprise fiber networks supporting multi-site customer connectivity. Consistently selected as lead engineer for high-priority and time-sensitive installations.

- Lead incident response for major network outages — root cause analysis, cross-functional remediation, post-incident hardening
- Identify and escalate security-relevant anomalies during fiber and switching operations, applying threat-awareness methodologies to carrier-level network monitoring (Cisco, Arista, Calix, ADVA)
- Configure and harden enterprise routing/switching environments, enforcing access controls and change management across the carrier network

**Stack:** Fiber design · DWDM · MPLS · BGP · OSPF · Cisco IOS · Arista EOS · Calix · ADVA

---

### Defense Systems Engineering *(2011 – 2021)*

Designated Electro-Optical Infrared SME on a Navy defense program — performed vulnerability analysis and security validation on defense-sector sensor systems under adversarial test conditions.

- Developed Python and MATLAB automation tooling that measurably improved test throughput and data accuracy across multiple concurrent defense R&D programs
- Operated within SCIF-adjacent environments, applying need-to-know access controls, operational security practices, and data handling protocols throughout the full program lifecycle
- Led cross-team test coordination under Scrum & Waterfall frameworks; served as senior escalation contact for high-complexity, security-sensitive technical issues

**Stack:** Python · MATLAB · Scrum · Waterfall · SCIF protocols · EO/IR systems

---

### Defense Infrastructure Administration *(2011)*

Administered user authentication, access controls, and network services for defense test infrastructure, enforcing least-privilege and audit requirements. Ensured data integrity and chain-of-custody on sensitive program deliverables.

---

### Defense Integration Labs *(2010 – 2011)*

Promoted to Lead Lab Technician within months. Maintained secure integration labs for weapons-system testing. Enforced access controls and operational security protocols. Mentored senior technicians on procedures, test readiness, and documentation standards.

---

### U.S. Navy — Aviation Electrician (E1–E6) *(2000 – 2010)*

10-year career with direct exposure to classified systems, physical security, and operational security requirements across shipboard, stateside, and test flight environments.

- Diagnosed and repaired analog, digital, RF, fiber-optic, and electrical circuits to component level
- Recognized as technical authority by test engineers and command leadership
- Supervised and trained 11 aviation electricians and avionics technicians in technical procedures, safety protocols, and maintenance management
